Three signals in chrome where they do no information work. The bars themselves are mono. The signal-allocation is inverted.

│                                                      │

Austere. Black ground, white type, no signals on the cover. The film is the signal. **Per-film accent appears only in the sting**, not on the static cover.

## DON'T ❻ — Festival cover with house signals

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  [black ground]                                      │
│                                                      │
│   ELECTRIC GREEN HEADLINE                            │
│                                                      │
│   مابين  ◆  Mabeen                                   │  ← bilingual divider
│   ━━━━━━━━━━━━ green underline                      │
│                                                      │
│   Ultramarine quote box: "A film about insurance"    │
│                                                      │
│   epink wordmark bottom-right                        │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The film context is austere. House signals do not belong here. The Mabeen cover wears the *film*, not the studio.

## Single most important DO/DON'T

> **DO:** mostly empty, one signal, one headline, one image.
> **DON'T:** four levels of hierarchy, three signals decorating, headline-quoting-itself.

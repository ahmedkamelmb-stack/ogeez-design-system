# OGeez Voice

The manifesto is the spine. This document is the operational pointer. Read [manifesto.md](./manifesto.md) first; every rule below cites it by paragraph.

## Language posture (¶7)

The studio writes in whichever language the idea is most native to. Egyptian for Egyptian ideas. Saudi for Saudi ideas. English when the brief asked in English and the idea answered back in it. MSA only on explicit request. Translation is not bilingual; sibling sentences are. Arabic and English never appear at unequal optical weight.

## Three-adjective defense test

Every line of client-facing copy has to name the three adjectives it's doing. If you can't name them, the line is doing nothing. Cut or rewrite.

## The bar test (¶1, ¶2)

Before any line ships, ask: would this read at the bar where a Cairo film sits next to a film made anywhere else and the room cannot tell the difference? If it reads as regional, the line is wrong.

## Prohibited phrases (cf. ¶6)

- *Full-service*, *content partner*, *end-to-end* — agency-shaped vocabulary; we are not that.
- *Award-winning* as opener — show the receipt in the third paragraph, not the first.
- *Innovative*, *cutting-edge*, *disruptive*, *storytelling*, *passionate*, *iconic*, *epic*, *vibes*, *game-changing*, *next-level* — dead agency words.
- *MENA market* / *MENA content* — say *Egypt + Gulf* or name the country.
- *Excited to announce* — dead opener.
- Named-canon references in client-facing work — absorb into craft, never name.
- *Filmmaker-led commercial studio with festival credibility and a bilingual bench* — retired with v4.

## Register selection

Brief-archetype → craft-move pairing lives in [register-matrix.md](./register-matrix.md). Diagnose the brief-type first, pull the move combo, then write. The house picks the register from the brief, not the brief from the register.

## Three overrides that win against everything

1. Absorb canon references. Never name them.
2. When offering alternatives, give exactly three — labeled by structural strategy, never by tone or risk.
3. KSA work auto-loads `agency/templates/saudi-creative-references.md` + `agency/templates/saudi-dialect-tone-guide.md`. Four-pattern checklist (*retrieve-don't-invent / refuse pan-Arab dilution / cast real lightly-directed / reframe category pain*) is the KSA quality bar — hit 3-of-4 before presenting.

## Single most important rule (cf. ¶4)

Loud frame, quiet copy. The image carries what the copy withholds. If the line shouts, cut it.

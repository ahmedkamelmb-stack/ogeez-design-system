# في مستوى.
*There is a bar.*

We started this to make work at the bar we measured by before anyone else did. The bar is not regional. The bar is not a category. The bar is where a film made in Cairo sits next to a film made anywhere else and the room cannot tell the difference — except the Cairo film has the better second of silence.

ما بنفصلش الإعلان عن الفيلم. The film discipline is what keeps the advertising honest. The advertising tempo is what keeps the film moving. The same hand signs both. The commercial work funds the cinema. The cinema holds the commercial work to its own bar. Both at the bar, or both refused.

We build for attention, not volume. A loud frame is not a loud cut. A loud cut is not a loud line. We let the image carry what the copy withholds. The second the audience gets to think is the second the work was built around.

يفضّل نكون دقيقين على ما نكون مبهرين. One helmet. Twenty helmets. A twenty-four-riyal note passed through eight specific hands. Never "thousands of homes delivered." Concrete nouns over abstract adjectives. The thing, not a word for the thing.

We refuse the slot. *Good regional* is a slot — we are not in it. *Full-service* is a price tier — we are not in it. *Content partner* is a job description — we did not take that job. *Award-winning* is something a client says about us in the third paragraph, never the first. We do not enter rooms with metaphors. We enter with a booth, a bench, a production calendar, and a name to sign.

The studio writes in whichever language the idea is most native to. Egyptian when the idea is Egyptian. Saudi when the idea is Saudi. English when the brief asked in English and the idea answered back in it. MSA only when somebody specifically asked. Translation is not bilingual. Sibling sentences are.

الشكل واحد. أربع إيدين وراه. Some hands are better in the room. Some hands are better with the cuts. Some hands run the calendar in Riyadh. Some hands run the slate in Cairo. The face the client meets is one. The work the client receives carries every hand.

We don't apologize for wanting to be paid for the work. The film and the campaign and the room cost money to keep open. We charge what the bar costs. Clients who pay for the bar are clients we keep. Clients who don't yet pay for the bar are clients we are pricing toward it. The money is the engine. The work is the reason.

There is a villa we are building toward. A pool, a ping-pong table, props from films we made, whiteboards, an editing suite, a music room, a production floor, a writing room, an AI bench, artists working on different projects in the same building on the same afternoon. The villa is not a metaphor. It is the working image. We are building the building.

The brand is the residue of the work. When the next film ships, the studio's surface shifts. The signature stays. The light changes.

Our cuts are too quiet. We treat that as a compliment. Our copy is too short. We treat that as a compliment. Our decks are too plain. We treat that as a compliment. The work that earned the room does not need to shout in it.

# عند المستوى، أو مرفوض.
*At the bar, or refused.*

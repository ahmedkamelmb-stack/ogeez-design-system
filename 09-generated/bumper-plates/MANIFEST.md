## How to use

1. The three plates are **register anchors**. They show the held-observational pacing, the warm-desaturated color story, the anti-swagger frame logic.
2. Edit them together with the OGeez wordmark and manifesto bookends (*في مستوى. / عند المستوى، أو مرفوض.*) in After Effects or Premiere.
3. Color grade pass: warm shadows, slight green-magenta cross-process, subtle film grain.
4. Drop in score (Hashem Shaheen recommended for original cue) or hold the silence.
5. Export at 1920×1080 mp4 + 9:16 social-trim (1.2s) + 1:1 (3s) variants.

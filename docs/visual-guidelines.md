## 0. The one-sentence brief

OGeeZ is a filmmaker-led commercial studio with festival credibility and a bilingual bench. The visual identity is **loud, vibrant, expressive — and deliberately shape-shifting.** Corporate-precision and punk-zine in the same envelope. The tension is the product, not a bug to smooth out.

Two lines anchor everything:
- **Brand Promise:** *A changing voice.*
- **Logo Concept:** *A cutting-edge new voice in the filmmaking industry that is loud, vibrant and expressive.*

If a design choice doesn't carry one of those two lines, it's wrong.

## 1. The most important rule before you design anything

**On-camera palette ≠ house palette.**

The Electric-Green / Ultramarine / Epink system is **deck wrapping** — used for OGeeZ's own identity, decks, stationery, social, recruiting, internal docs. It is NOT the on-camera color of OGeeZ film/commercial work for clients.

On-camera color is **client-tier-deterministic**, picked per brief:
- **Corporate / institutional** (banks, government, B2B): restrained, muted, black + single accent.
- **Consumer / pop** (F&B, telco, retail): saturation up, pattern-forward.
- **Heritage / patriotic** (national days, anniversaries): earth tones, art-direction-heavy.

Translate this for any visual you make for OGeeZ itself → use the house palette at full strength. Anything you make to represent OGeeZ's *work for a client* → respect the client's tier.

## 3. Logo system

### 3.1 Anatomy
The primary mark is a two-element lockup:

1. **Wordmark** — speech-bubble shaped "O!GZ" in **OGZ Electric Green**, sitting on an Epink drop-shadow with a subtle white offset. Black letterforms inside the green bubble. The exclamation mark is a black diamond/lozenge. Arabic **ستوديوز** sits beneath the wordmark in black, hand-drawn brush-style script.
2. **Mouth icon** — green lips with white tooth row, black mouth interior, airplane silhouette across the teeth, sparkle/star, **magenta-highlighted single tooth**, red tongue, three green shout-lines. The mouth functions as standalone visual element AND as the locked companion to the wordmark, connected by a small "speech-tail" line.

### 3.4 Clear space
Minimum clear space around the primary lockup = **1X**, where 1X = half the height of the mouth icon. Apply on all four sides.

### 3.5 Incorrect usage
Do **not**:
- Alter colors of the wordmark or the typeface inside it.
- Place the lockup on backgrounds with insufficient contrast.
- Skew the axis (the lockup is always at 90°, never tilted).
- Stretch or condense — scale proportionally only.
- Add additional drop shadows (the Epink shadow is part of the mark; no further shadow).
- Re-color the typeface inside the speech bubble.
- Redraw or re-vectorize the lockup. Use the master EPS / PDF.

## 4. The mouth as a parametric system

This is the most important thing about OGeeZ visual identity that yesterday's brief got wrong. Treat the mouth not as one illustration but as a **parametric component**.

### 4.1 Fixed anatomy (never change)
Every mouth carries the same internal anatomy:
- Outer lip silhouette (color shifts; shape never).
- Two small white highlight glints on the upper lip.
- White tooth row (top + bottom).
- Black "void" interior at top of mouth.
- **Airplane silhouette** across the teeth (the visual joke — the mouth has *bitten* an airplane).
- Sparkle / star icon top-right interior.
- **Magenta-highlighted single tooth** — *always magenta*, even on the Epink mouth. Magenta is a fixed accent, not derived from the lip color.
- Red tongue.
- Three "shout" lines emanating from the left side, color-matched to the lip.

### 4.2 12 color variants (the master library)

### 4.3 Mouth treatment modes (5 patterns observed in Applications)
The same mouth gets layered creative treatments on covers, posters, and motion graphics:

1. **Flat** — default. Solid color, no shadow. Lockup, business cards, letterhead, Instagram posts.
2. **Duotone shadow / echo** — second-color offset shadow behind the mouth (e.g. mint background + Epink mouth + green offset shadow). Presentation slide 6.
3. **Motion-trail** — extended fading shadow trailing toward one direction, suggesting speed/sound. WeTransfer backgrounds.
4. **Bleed-off-edge** — mouth is cropped past slide/canvas edge, with ~25–40% of the silhouette beyond the bounds. Presentation separator slide 4.
5. **Photo-into-mask** — a photograph is clipped *inside* the mouth silhouette as a masked vignette. Presentation separator slide 7.

Use these treatments on the *master mouth*. Do not redraw mood-state mouths.

### 4.4 Mood lockups (Action!/Standby/IWD/Halloween/etc.)
Per Ahmed (2026-04-27): these mood-state lockups exist **only as rendered images** in the original brand manual. No production source files. If a future calendar moment needs one, it is a one-off illustration done in the same construction language — never automated, never AI-regenerated. Closed open question.

### 5.2 Secondary palette (extends from the mouth library)
The 12 mouth colors above also serve as a wider house accent palette — used for slide flood backgrounds, business-card backs, mobile wallpaper grounds. Anchor swatches: orange (#F59A1F), mint (#28D9A8), sky blue (#1FBDFF), yellow (#FFD500), coral (#F5BEB7), cream (#E8D6A0), khaki (#C4B560), grey (#BFBFBF).

### 5.3 Bar-chart palettes (observed in deck samples)
Charts use a 5-color palette tonally tuned to the slide background:

- **On Epink coral background** → black + light blue + Epink + grey + magenta.
- **On sky blue background** → black + cream + tan + dark brown + khaki.

Rule: chart palette is one tone family + one Epink-or-equivalent disruptor + black for the strongest data series. Don't deploy the full primary palette in a chart.

### 5.4 Color rules
- **Full strength always** — never tint primary palette to pastels.
- **Two-color tension is the default** — Green field + Ultramarine type, Ultramarine field + white type, Epink as the disruptor.
- **Black is structural** — holds the typography and the wordmark interior; not an accent.
- **Reserve Epink for impact** — the punctuation mark of the layout, not the field.

## 6. Typography

### 6.1 English — Modern Era (familytype.co/#modernera)
Sans-serif. Large x-height, low stroke contrast, beveled stroke joints, contrasted wider circulars. 12 weights from Light to Heavy + matching italics. Modern Era Mono in 4 styles.

**The font files are bundled** at `Brand Identity/Applications/Presentation design/Modern Era Family.zip`. Use these — do not source elsewhere.

Default ladder:
- **Display / cover headlines:** Modern Era Black or ExtraBold
- **Slide / page headlines:** Modern Era Bold
- **Subheads:** Modern Era Bold or Medium
- **Body copy:** Modern Era Medium or Regular
- **Captions / metadata / footers:** Modern Era Regular (small, ~10pt)
- **All-caps stationery names:** Modern Era Black for first name, Regular for last name (per business-card layout)

### 6.2 Arabic — Neue Frutiger Arabic (Nadine Chahine / Monotype)
10 weights from Ultra Light to Extra Black. Frutiger LT 45 Light → 55 Roman → 65 Bold → 75 Black is the standing ladder.

Pair Modern Era and Neue Frutiger Arabic at **equal optical weight** — not equal point size. Arabic and English are siblings, not subtitle/caption pairs. If English is Bold, Arabic is Bold. If English is Light, Arabic is Light.

### 6.3 Type rules
- Headlines big, never timid.
- One level of hierarchy per slide. No four-level pyramids.
- Arabic typesets right-to-left and reads at a slightly different rhythm than English — let it breathe, don't force matching line lengths.
- Stationery uses ALL CAPS on names (`ALHARETH ALBELAIHED`); decks use mixed case (`Presentation Name/Title`). Different voices for different surfaces.

## 7. Layout & grid — the presentation system

The OGeeZ deck is a 9-layout system. Master file: `Brand Identity/Applications/Presentation design/OGZ Presesentation slides.pdf` + the editable PPTX.

### 7.1 The 9 canonical layouts

**L1 — Cover (mouth-hero)**
White background. Full-color green mouth occupying the right ~45% of the slide. Title in Modern Era Black, mixed case, large, left-aligned around 30% from left edge. Subhead/name below in Modern Era Regular. Date in Modern Era Regular, light grey, bottom-left. *No wordmark on the cover — the mouth alone carries identity.*

**L2 — Headline + body (color flood)**
Full-bleed Electric Green (or other primary) background. Small mouth lockup top-left. "Headline" in Modern Era Bold (black). Body in Modern Era Regular (black). Footer "Presentation Title - October 20, 2023" in Modern Era Regular small bottom-left.

**L3 — Separator (image overlay)**
Full-bleed photograph (talent in colored light, music-video grade frame). White Modern Era Black headline overlaid + Modern Era Regular subhead. Footer in Modern Era Regular small bottom-left.

**L4 — Separator (mouth bleeds off edge)**
Full-bleed Ultramarine (or other primary) background. Massive Electric Green mouth on the right, **cropped past the slide edge** — ~30–40% of the mouth past the canvas bounds. Headline in Modern Era Black white, left ~20% from edge. Subhead in Modern Era Regular white below.

**L5 — Data slide (pastel ground)**
Soft secondary-palette flood (coral, sky blue, etc.). Small mouth top-left. Headline + body in left third (Modern Era Bold + Regular). 5-color bar chart in right two-thirds, palette tuned to background.

**L6 — Separator (mouth with duotone shadow)**
Color flood (e.g. mint). Massive mouth in second color (e.g. Epink) with offset shadow trail in a third color (e.g. green). Headline in Modern Era Black, subhead Modern Era Regular, both black or white per contrast.

**L7 — Separator (photo-in-mouth-mask)**
Color flood. Photograph clipped inside the mouth silhouette as a masked vignette. Headline white Modern Era Black, large, centered or offset.

**L8 — Data slide (saturated ground)**
Saturated secondary flood (sky blue, etc.). Same anatomy as L5 but bar chart palette shifts to earth tones.

**L9 — Image-right (text-left)**
White background. Small mouth top-left. Headline Modern Era Bold + body Modern Era Regular in left half. Full-bleed photograph (~45% width) on right edge.

### 7.2 Footer rhythm
Every slide carries a Modern Era Regular ~10pt footer bottom-left: `Presentation Title - DD MMM YYYY`. Color: black on light grounds, white on dark grounds, dark gray on white.

### 7.3 Slide grid
Underlying 12-column grid with generous left margin and a right content well. The cover and separator layouts use the right two-thirds for the visual element (mouth or image).

## 8. Visual moves — five recurring creative treatments

These are the patterns that make OGeeZ visual material look like OGeeZ visual material. They're orthogonal to layout — any layout can deploy any of these.

| # | Move | Where it shows up | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Mouth bleeds off-edge** | Deck separator slides, posters | Mouth is intentionally cropped 25–40% past the canvas bound. Confidence move; signals scale. |
| 2 | **Duotone mouth shadow** | Posters, separator slides | Mouth in one color with offset shadow in second color. Static but feels kinetic. |
| 3 | **Motion-trail** | WeTransfer backgrounds, motion intros, hero stills | Extended fading shadow trailing behind the mouth toward bottom-left or bottom-right. Speed cue. |
| 4 | **Photo-into-mouth-mask** | Deck separator slide 7, key visuals | Photograph clipped inside the mouth silhouette. Music-video grade frame inside a vinyl-sticker shape. |
| 5 | **Co-branding × mark** | Instagram posts, partnership announcements | `OGZ × [Partner]` with a green "×" multiplier between the OGeeZ wordmark and the partner mark. Source example: OGZ × Coca-Cola. |

## 9. Photographic & footage treatment

Master examples in `Brand Identity/Applications/Presentation design/PNGS/` and the Instagram template.

For OGeeZ-self-promotion (NOT for client work — see §1):
- **Lit in saturated single-color light** (Ultramarine, Epink, Green spill). Talent shot under colored light, not graded to a tint in post. Production choice, not finishing.
- **Subjects centered or compressed at one edge.**
- **Tight on the face / hands / object.** Wide environmental shots are rare.
- **Real talent, lightly directed.** No stock-feeling smiles.
- **Cinematic ratio.** 2.35:1 or 1.85:1 framing preferred; 16:9 acceptable; never 1:1 for hero work.

For BTS / documentary / Instagram work:
- **Black-and-white documentary photography.** Single brand-color (Electric Green) used selectively for: title text, the mouth illustration overlay, and any co-branding mark. Everything else stays B&W.
- Title set in Modern Era Black, ALL CAPS, large, top-left.

## 11. Quick do's & don'ts

**Do**
- Use the master files. They exist. Don't redraw or re-vectorize.
- Treat the mouth as parametric: 12 colors × 5 treatment modes.
- Pair Arabic and English at equal optical weight.
- Hold the corporate-precision + punk-zine tension. Don't smooth it.
- Tune chart palettes to the slide background, not to a fixed deck palette.
- Set headlines big.
- Light photography in single saturated colors, not gels-everywhere.

**Don't**
- Tint the primary colors to pastels.
- Skew, stretch, drop-shadow, or re-color the lockup.
- Redraw the mouth — color-shift the master, don't regenerate.
- Set Arabic smaller than English or as a translation underneath.
- Apply the house palette to client on-camera work (§1).
- Generate mood-state mouths from prompts. They're rendered images only.
- Paint over the brand's strangeness. The strangeness is the asset.

## 14. Hand-off summary for Claude Design

If you only read one thing: OGeeZ's identity is **two opposite-feeling things at the same time** — a corporate-spec system that holds (logo grid, color HEX, type pairing, 9 deck layouts) AND a punk-zine sensibility that won't sit still (12-color mouth library, full-strength neon, talent lit in saturated single-color light, mouth-bleeds-off-edge moves).

# Film Context — *OGeez Presents*

## Purpose

`data-context="film"` is for OGeez-as-filmmaker, not OGeez-as-commercial-studio. This is the ***OGeez Presents* surface** — the studio's name and wordmark stay constant; per-film artwork is bespoke, drawn from the film's grading, palette, photography. **A24-precedent.**

The seam between the commercial body and the filmmaker body is the brand's most valuable real estate. The film context is where the filmmaker side lives. Don't smooth the seam, don't fake the seam.

Use it for:
- Mabeen
- Milliona
- Halayeb
- Timo
- Home Sweet Home
- الشلة الأصلية shorts
- Festival submissions (Cannes, Sundance, SXSW, El Gouna, Red Sea)
- Theatrical bumpers
- Director treatment covers when the work is film-first
- film related stuff

## *OGeez Presents* surface — how it works

**Constants** (every film carries these):
- *"OGeez Presents"* mark in the studio wordmark
- *© OGeez Studios* attribution
- The base sting variant (one of festival / theatrical / streaming / social-trim)

**Variables** (per-film bespoke, drawn from the film's own visual world):
- Per-film key art
- Per-film palette (taken from the film's grading)
- Per-film photography
- Per-film typography accent (within the spine — Modern Era + IBM Plex Sans Arabic stay)
- One bespoke parameter inside the chosen sting (e.g., the color of the mouth-mark's accent line at the festival sting's "settle" moment)

**The contract:** one base sting + one bespoke parameter per project. **No more.** This keeps the system coherent while allowing per-film identity. Documented in the project's treatment doc at delivery.

## Visual register (`[data-context="film"]`)

- Black ground.
- White type.
- One accent at most (defaults to muted accent; can match the film's color story for the festival sting).
- Sparse use of mouth mark — never as headline.
- Heavy restraint.
- Texture allowed only when it feels like material, not decoration.

## Motion register

- Festival sting: 4 seconds, silent, austere.
- Theatrical sting: 8 seconds, scored.
- Streaming sting: 3 seconds.
- Social trim: 1.2 seconds, vertical.
- No bouncing type.
- No sales-deck rhythm.
- Held beats are allowed and encouraged. Match-cuts honored.

## Voice register

Filmmaker voice, not agency voice.

Use:
- Image logic.
- Withheld moments.
- Performance notes.
- Cut causality.

Avoid:
- Client-benefit language.
- Campaign jargon.
- "Premium film" claims.
- Reference-name dropping.

## The cumulative-residue logic lives here, not in the studio's identity

V3 is explicit: the studio's *core* identity (palette, wordmark deployment, layout grammar) is stable across films. The cumulative-residue model — where each shipped feature shapes the brand's surface for 18-36 months — applies only to the **OGeez Presents** surface. Mabeen wears Mabeen. Halayeb wears Halayeb. The studio's house identity does not mutate per film.

A24-precedent: per-film bespoke posters, consistent wordmark.

## Single most important rule (film context)

**The brand wears the film, not the other way around.** The *OGeez Presents* mark stays constant. Everything else is the film's.

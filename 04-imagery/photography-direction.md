# Photography Direction

How OGeez photographs and selects imagery. **The image carries what the copy withholds** (manifesto ¶3).

## Register

Patient. Observational. Sun-washed. Low-saturation. Anti-stock.

Reference filmmakers (absorbed, never named to clients): Mohamed Khan, Youssef Chahine, Roy Andersson, Luis Buñuel, Yorgos Lanthimos, A24's house photography. The house's craft tradition.

## What the photos look like

- **Real places, real light.** Sun coming in low through a narrow window. Golden hour on a brutalist exterior. Dawn on the Corniche.
- **Faces often withheld.** Subject back to camera, or hand-only, or silhouette. The face is reserved for moments where the face is the subject.
- **Horizon disciplined.** Classical thirds. The horizon either at upper or lower third — almost never at center.
- **One subject per frame.** The clapperboard. The figure walking. The empty desk.
- **Anti-glamour.** Even when shooting a celebrity, the lighting stays naturalistic. No hero lighting. No commercial polish.
- **Color story.** Warm neutrals dominate. One signal accent at most (the green sticker on the clapper, the magenta filing cabinet at the edge, the ultramarine note in the river). The signal is *in* the world, not painted on top.

## Lighting

- Natural where possible.
- Mixed daylight + tungsten = OK (the warmth is the point).
- Hard shadows allowed and welcomed.
- High-key glamour lighting = banned.
- Ring lights = banned.
- Beauty dishes = banned.
- The flat-lit influencer-portrait look = banned.

## Composition

- Wide where the place is the subject.
- Tight where the object is the subject.
- Medium-shots avoided unless they're medium-for-a-reason.
- Frames hold for a moment longer than the eye expects.

## What we never source from

- **iStockPhoto, Shutterstock, Getty default packs.** The packs are anti-OGeez. If we have to use stock, source from Stocksy United or commissioned photographers.
- **Unsplash MENA-themed packs.** Saturated tourism, polished Dubai-skyline, "exotic" colorism — all the failure modes.
- **AI-generated images that look AI-generated.** If the AI is a tool to generate a mood reference, fine. If the AI image *ships* in a client deck, it has to read as photography, not as Stable-Diffusion-default.
- **Glossy product shots with floating objects on gradient backgrounds.** Industry-default consumer-pop photography is not OGeez consumer-pop photography.

## Per-context photography

### `house` (default)

Sun-washed warm, low-saturation, observational. Use the four mood references as register anchor.

### `house-dark`

Same register, dark-grounded surfaces. Photos can be high-contrast b&w or deeply shadowed color. Cinema-noir affinity.

### `film` (OGeez Presents surface)

Use the actual film's photography — Mabeen still, Halayeb still, Milliona still, الشلة الأصلية still. **The brand wears the film.** The studio's house photography retreats; the film's photography leads.

### `oncamera-corporate`

Restrained, deeply lit, naturalistic. Bank Aljazira / NHC / Sign — the photography matches the client's tier. Often interior, often architectural, often with one human in the lower third going about work.

### `oncamera-consumer`

Saturation up. The brand's photography for a Drinkle product reveal lives in the consumer-tier visual world. Color flood, character pop, bigger smiles. Still warm, still naturalistic — but the dial moves up.

### `oncamera-heritage`

Earth tones, period-accurate, sand / ochre / olive palette. Real places, real heat. National-day briefs, cultural anniversaries. Photography leans toward the documentary.

## Single most important rule

> **The photo carries the slide. The slide doesn't carry the photo.**

If a slide needs three callouts, two arrows, and a corner badge to make a photo "work" — the photo is wrong. Find a different photo. Or commission one. Or shoot it.

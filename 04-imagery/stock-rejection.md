# Stock Rejection List

What OGeez never sources from. Read before commissioning, licensing, or generating imagery.

## The principle

The image carries the brand. If the image says "we are a regional content studio," the brand says "we are a regional content studio." If we want the brand to read at the bar, the image has to read at the bar.

## What we never use

### From stock libraries

- **Shutterstock default-pack imagery.** The "MENA business meeting around a tablet" shot. The "Saudi vision 2030" generic skyline. The "Cairo street with motion blur" tourist shot.
- **iStock pack-of-the-month.** Anything that 100 other regional brands have used.
- **Getty's editorial regional pack.** Often great for news, almost never for brand.
- **Unsplash "Middle East" search results above the first page.** First page sometimes useful as mood — not for ship.
- **Adobe Stock "MENA brand" packs.** Marketed to agencies; that's the tell.
- **Pexels pack imagery.** Same problem.

### From AI generation

- **AI images that look AI-generated.** Pearlescent skin, six-fingered hands, weirdly lit eyes, "almost-real but uncanny."
- **Stable Diffusion v1 / v2 outputs without inpainting cleanup.**
- **Midjourney's default "cinematic" style without prompt restraint.** The over-sharpened, over-graded, "every shot looks like a movie poster" failure mode.
- **Generic AI portraiture.** Particularly faces — the studio uses real talent, lightly directed.

### From influencer-pack visuals

- Ring-lit flat-lit Instagram aesthetic.
- "Influencer office" backgrounds with hanging plants and brass desk lamps.
- The pristine Dubai-startup-Mariana-Trench aesthetic.

### From corporate template libraries

- Canva business-deck stock.
- Slidesgo, SlidesCarnival, similar template-derived imagery.
- "Successful businessman" / "happy team" / "diverse meeting" stock.

## What we use instead

| Source | When | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Stocksy United | Last resort if commission not feasible | Higher curation than Shutterstock; still verify register match |
| Commissioned photography | Default for client work | Real talent, real places, lightly directed |
| AI-generated mood references | Internal mood / pitch / pre-production reference | Used as register anchor, NOT for ship — except where AI is the medium of the work itself |
| The film's own stills | `film` context | Mabeen / Halayeb / Milliona / الشلة الأصلية stills |
| Public domain archival imagery | Heritage / cultural briefs | Egyptian National Library archive; Saudi National Library; King Faisal Foundation archive — verify rights |

## The "is it AI?" disclosure

When the studio generates imagery for client decks (mood plates, pitch references), the artifact is internal mood material, not ship. **Ship imagery is photographed unless the brief explicitly invites AI as the medium.**

When the brief invites AI (which Zain Shabab Revamp may, given the AI-bench positioning), AI imagery can ship — but it must:
1. Be disclosed as AI in the deck (small caption, lower right).
2. Read as deliberate, not as a stock substitute.
3. Carry the same register anchors as the photographic work (low saturation, observational frame, anti-glossy).

## Single most important rule

> **If the image is "fine, generic, regional" — kill it.** The brand's bar test applies to imagery as much as to copy.

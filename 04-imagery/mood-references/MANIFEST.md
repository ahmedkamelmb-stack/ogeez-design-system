## How to use

These are **register anchors**, not deck-ready stock. They show:
- Color story: warm desaturated, sun-washed, anti-stock.
- Frame logic: classical thirds, observational, faces withheld.
- One signal accent **in the world**, not painted on top.

When commissioning a real shoot or selecting from a library, match this register.

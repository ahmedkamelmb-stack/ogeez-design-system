## Identity in one breath

OGeeZ Studios. Filmmaker-led commercial studio. Cairo + Riyadh. Bilingual (Arabic + English, sibling not subtitle). The mark is a green speech-bubble wordmark with an Epink magenta drop-shadow and a green mouth that has bitten an airplane.

## What you must know before generating anything

**1. Pick a context.** `house` for OGeeZ-self artifacts (decks, social, recruiting, stationery). `oncamera.{corporate|consumer|heritage}` for client work. The house palette and on-camera palette are different systems. Mixing them is the most common failure mode.

**2. The mouth is parametric.** 12 lip colors × 5 treatments (flat, duotone-shadow, motion-trail, bleed-off-edge, photo-into-mask) = the entire visual language. The mouth anatomy never changes. The magenta tooth highlight is fixed — it stays magenta even when the mouth is Epink.

**3. Use the master files.** Logo, mouth, font — all production-ready in `assets/`. Don't redraw, don't AI-regenerate, don't substitute a stock font.

**4. Mood-state mouths (Action!/Standby/Halloween) are rendered images only.** Never generate new ones from prompts. If a calendar moment needs one, it goes back to a human designer.

**5. Voice and visual hold the same tension.** The corporate-spec system is real (12-column grid, type ladder, color HEX) AND the punk-zine sensibility is real (full-strength neon, talent in saturated single-color light, mouth bleeds off-edge). Design that picks one side over the other is wrong.

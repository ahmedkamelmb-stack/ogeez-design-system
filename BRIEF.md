# BRIEF — OGeez Brand System

A 1-page distillation. The full canon lives in `00-spine/`, `01-tokens/`, `02-typography/`, `03-grid-and-layout/`.

## Identity in one breath

OGeez is a Cairo + Riyadh studio held to **one bar** — the bar where a Cairo film sits next to a film made anywhere else and the room cannot tell the difference, except the Cairo film has the better second of silence. Same hand on the film and the campaign. Bilingual: idea-native, neither language a translation of the other.

**Client-facing positioning:** *Attention, not volume.*
**Manifesto bookends:** *في مستوى. / عند المستوى، أو مرفوض.*

## What you must know before generating anything

**1. One signal per artifact in `house` context.** The three signals — Electric Green / Ultramarine / Epink — are the brand's voltage. Substrate (paper / ink / stone / slate / cream) carries the rest.

**2. Pick a context.** Six contexts: `house` (default), `house-dark`, `film` (OGeez Presents — austere), `oncamera-corporate`, `oncamera-consumer`, `oncamera-heritage`. The on-camera palette is **not** the house palette.

**3. Pick a posture.** `client.status=first_time` → 11-section template (L1). `client.status=repeat` → idea-first (L10). No studio chrome on slide 1 of an idea-first deck.

**4. The wordmark is the public face. The mouth is a discipline mark.** Sparingly used. Magenta tooth and red tongue NEVER change.

**5. Motion is held, observational, anti-swagger.** Three easings (`pull`, `hold`, `lift`). Six durations. The "second of silence" is structural.

**6. Bilingual is idea-native.** Egyptian for Egyptian ideas. Saudi for Saudi. English when the brief asked. MSA only on explicit request. Translation is not bilingual; sibling sentences are.

**7. Grain/texture is opt-in only.** Never default. Use only when it's evidence the work was made — never to look "made-by-hand."

**8. Modern Era + Neue Frutiger Arabic. If unavailable, drop to system-ui — never Geist or Inter.**

## Single most important rule

> **Look less designed, not more.** Every page asks: *would this read at the bar?* If a design choice is doing decorative work, cut it.
